# Support

Wesley is pre-1.0 and maintained on a best-effort basis.

- **Questions and design discussion:**
  <https://github.com/flyingrobots/wesley/discussions>
- **Bugs:** <https://github.com/flyingrobots/wesley/issues>. Include the version
  (`wesley --version`) or commit, the command you ran, what you expected, and
  what happened. A schema small enough to paste is the fastest route to a fix.
- **Security:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not file a public issue.
