# EVIDENCE

## Scope

`EVIDENCE` covers evidence maps, provenance, HOLMES/WATSON/MORIARTY,
certification, counterfactual analysis, and the boundary between collected
facts and Wesley-native judgment.

## Guards

- `evidence-truth`
- `provenance-visibility`
- `docs-runtime-honesty`
- `governance-boundaries`

## Standing playback questions

- Can the system show why a score, verdict, or gate result exists?
- Would a reviewer trust the evidence path without having to guess what was
  synthesized or assumed?
