# Retired: Continuum Contract Bundle Atlas

## What was retired

The cool-ideas backlog item `RUNTIME_continuum-contract-bundle-atlas.md` was
retired from Wesley's active queue.

## Why

The item was written against the older Continuum/Echo/TTD built-in command and
generator era. Its evidence pointed at removed Wesley-local surfaces such as
`compile-ttd`, `bundle-echo`, and the Echo generator package.

Continuum may still want a contract-family atlas, but that is coordination
truth for the Continuum repo, not a Wesley product surface.

## Reopen condition

Reopen only as a generic Wesley artifact inspection surface, or in Continuum as
a Continuum-owned contract-family atlas grounded in current schema and registry
truth.
