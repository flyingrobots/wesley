# Retired: Continuum Generated-Leg Certificates

## What was retired

The cool-ideas backlog item `EVIDENCE_continuum-generated-leg-certificates.md`
was retired from Wesley's active queue.

## Why

The useful idea was per-leg compatibility evidence, but the concrete card was
anchored to removed Continuum/Echo built-ins and old witness commands. In the
domain-empty architecture, product-specific generated legs belong to external
modules and owning repos.

## Reopen condition

Reopen only after the module capability evidence model is concrete enough to
define generic artifact certificates without naming Echo, TTD, or Continuum as
built-in Wesley targets.
