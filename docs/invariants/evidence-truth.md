# evidence-truth

## Invariant statement

Scores, certs, gates, and readiness judgments must be backed by the strongest
real evidence the surface actually has. Missing, coarse, or provisional
evidence must be labeled honestly instead of being presented as certified fact.

## Preserved when

- evidence-backed claims resolve to real artifacts, spans, digests, or exported
  facts
- Holmes-family tools distinguish exact, whole-file, coarse, and missing
  evidence honestly
- certified claims stay verifier-facing and challengeable

## Violated when

- placeholder synthesis or coarse citations are treated as if they were exact
  proof
- scores or verdicts suppress the weakness of their supporting evidence
- docs imply a trust level the bundle or cert cannot actually justify

## How to check

- look for exact spans, digests, verifier coverage, and tests that distinguish
  weak from strong evidence
- inspect report output for explicit evidence trust or equivalent honesty
  signals
- challenge any workflow that upgrades advisory evidence into governed proof
  without new supporting facts
